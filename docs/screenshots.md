# Release screenshot checklist

Do not use old MultiCore images or design mockups as screenshots of the shipping client.

Before a public release, capture the final Windows x64 candidate at 100% and 125% scale with:

- a dedicated test account and non-personal display name;
- synthetic or public-safe subscription names;
- no email address, Telegram handle, token, subscription URL, HWID, IP address, log secret, or notification from another application;
- Home, Account menu, Subscriptions, expired state, Settings, and Quick Panel;
- both connected and disconnected states where useful;
- consistent version and date data.

Review every image at full resolution before committing it. Store approved images under `docs/assets/terrieflow/` and update the README only after that review.
